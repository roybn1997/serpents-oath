We keep your account and gameplay data for as long as your account exists. Chat messages are kept only as long as needed to show them and to look into reports of abuse, and in any case for no longer than 12 months. Bug reports are kept until they are handled and for up to 24 months. Security records (such as failed sign-in attempts) are kept for a short time, usually no more than 30 days. We protect your data with reasonable measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords stored only as hashes, access to each player's data limited to that player by the database itself, and the launcher's sign-in token kept in your operating system's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your computer safe.
